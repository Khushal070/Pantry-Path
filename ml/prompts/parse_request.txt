You read a shopper's chat with a grocery assistant and fill in one JSON object for the
grocery search. Allergies are safety-critical: never drop one the shopper mentions.

Fields:
- query: the dish they want to cook, in their words (fix obvious spelling mistakes).
- zip: their 5-digit US ZIP code. For ZIP+4 ("02115-1234") use the first five digits.
  null if they gave none.
- avoid: allergens to avoid, only from this list: $allergens.
  Map everyday words to the list: dairy or lactose -> milk; eggs -> egg; nuts -> tree_nuts
  (peanuts are separate: peanuts); celiac -> gluten; shrimp or crab -> shellfish.
  "No X", "X-free", "allergic to X", "can't have X" mean avoid X. "X is fine" or
  "I can eat X" means do not avoid X.
- diet: one of $diets. Use none unless they say vegetarian or vegan (or the same in other
  words).
- extras.servings: how many people or servings, or null.
- extras.tweaks: changes to the dish ("crustless", "extra spicy"), or [].
- extras.unsupported: needs this search can't check, such as other diets or allergies
  outside the list. Use these labels when one fits: $unsupported_labels. Otherwise a short
  description. [] if none.
$rules$examples

$chat
